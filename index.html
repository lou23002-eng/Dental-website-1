Meet the Doctor

Dr. Tate Weller

Dr. Tate Weller, periodontist in Hickory, NC, offers advanced dental techniques and procedures to treat periodontal disease. Dr. Weller attended the University of Utah dental school with periodontal training at Augusta University. He offers treatments such as bone grafting, gum grafting, and sinus lift procedures.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Weller enjoys hiking, playing disc golf, and spending time with his wife and 4 children.

Transformative Periodontal Solutions

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth with a permanent solution that looks and functions like natural teeth. Our state-of-the-art implant technology and surgical expertise ensure long-lasting results and exceptional outcomes. From single tooth replacement to full mouth restoration, we create confident, beautiful smiles.

Gum Grafting

Address gum recession with precision graft procedures that restore gum tissue, strengthen tooth support, and enhance your smile's appearance. Our minimally invasive techniques use advanced surgical methods to achieve natural-looking, long-lasting results with minimal post-operative discomfort.

Periodontal Disease Treatment

From early gingivitis to advanced periodontitis, we provide comprehensive treatment to restore oral health and prevent tooth loss. Our customized approach combines professional care with patient education, ensuring lasting results and a healthier future for your smile.

Bone Grafts

Enhance jaw structure and create a strong foundation for dental implants with expert bone grafting procedures. Our team uses advanced materials and precision techniques to rebuild bone safely and effectively, supporting long-term oral health and improved implant outcomes.
