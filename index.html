Bright Smile Dental provides professional dental care including cleanings, fillings, whitening, and preventative care.

About Our Clinic

Bright Smile Dental focuses on preventative and restorative dentistry. Regular dental visits help prevent cavities, gum disease, and tooth decay.

Dentists recommend brushing twice daily, flossing once per day, and visiting a dental professional every six months for a cleaning and exam.

Our Services

Dental Cleanings

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing alone cannot remove.

Teeth Whitening

Whitening treatments brighten teeth by removing stains caused by coffee, tea, and other foods.

Cavity Fillings

Fillings restore teeth damaged by decay and prevent further damage.

Preventative Care

Preventative dentistry includes exams, x-rays, fluoride treatments, and patient education.
